Solo Developer

Leo Alfred A. Mosquera

Leo is a solo software developer developing the SeeYou-Debt application. Seeing the lack of reliable software for financial management needs, he thought of this project, patterned after his personal needs. While current technology offers some solutions to overall problems, its broadness sometimes misses the specific solution for specific problems. Sometimes applications become too cluttered trying to do everything in just one app. Budgeting software is one example of this. Equipped with the vision to help everyone who has experienced what he has experienced, Leo was determined to provide a solution to the specific problem of managing debts and seeing a way out of it. That's how SeeYou-Debt came to life.
